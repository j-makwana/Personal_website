01BoxmateFounder2024 – present

A storage marketplace for students

Students at UW-Madison need somewhere to keep their things over the summer. Boxmate connects them with local hosts who store their boxes. I started it, set the pricing, and led a team of about 10 to build the website, backend and host app.

boxmate.store ↗
What we learned
230+ survey responses and interviews showed students were price sensitive, freshmen hadn’t heard of us, and many wanted pickup and delivery.
What I changed
Pricing went from per item, to box bundles, to three flat tiers at $29, $39 and $49. I also added pickup and delivery.
What happened
150+ students used Boxmate, and the host app had 100+ downloads from people interested in hosting.

02USEMCOElectrical engineerMar – Jul 2026

Control panels, and a tool for their paperwork

I designed control panels for client orders. Then I built a tool that puts together the datasheet package each job needs, which engineers had been doing by hand.

The panel work

  1. Scope

    Worked with sales to turn client requirements into a panel order.

  2. Revisions

    Handled client changes through submittal and resubmittal.

  3. Release

    Cleared compliance and layout review, then sent the EPLAN schematics to the shop floor for assembly.

The cutsheet builder

Every job needs its datasheets, in the right order.

The tool reads the EPLAN parts list, matches each part to its datasheet, and uses Claude to check the order against our rules. An engineer accepts or rejects each suggestion before the PDF is built. Corrections go back into the rules.

Python · Next.js · Claude API

Used by
All six engineers, across about 18 jobs a week.
Saves
10–15 minutes per job, and it flags missing datasheets.
Later
Added search over past jobs to reuse schematic sections and track progress.
